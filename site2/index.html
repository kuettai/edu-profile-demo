BIGBANG

Kings of K-Pop

B

IGBANG is a South Korean group formed by YG Entertainment in 2006. Widely regarded as one of the most influential acts in K-pop history, they pioneered the genre's global expansion with genre-bending music spanning hip-hop, EDM, R&B, and pop. With over 150 million records sold, multiple world tours, and countless awards, BIGBANG redefined what a K-pop group could be — artists, producers, fashion icons, and cultural ambassadors.
