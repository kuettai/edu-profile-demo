Catan

🏝️ Trade, Build, Settle — The World's Favorite Board Game

🌍 About the Game

Originally published as The Settlers of Catan in 1995 by Klaus Teuber, Catan has sold over 40 million copies in 40+ languages. Players collect and trade resources — wood, brick, sheep, wheat, and ore — to build roads, settlements, and cities on the island of Catan. Simple to learn, endlessly strategic, and guaranteed to end friendships (temporarily). It's the gateway game that launched millions into the world of modern board gaming.

🎲 How to Play

1Setup

Build the island with random hex tiles. Place your first 2 settlements and roads.

2Roll

Roll two dice. Everyone with settlements on matching numbers collects resources.

3Trade

Negotiate with other players. "I'll give you 2 sheep for 1 ore!" Deals get wild.

4Build

Spend resources to build roads, settlements, cities, or buy development cards.

5Win!

First player to reach 10 victory points wins. 🏆
