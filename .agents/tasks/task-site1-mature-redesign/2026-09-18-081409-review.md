# Site1 mature editorial redesign (v1 review)

Site1's personal-profile page was reworked from a playful, pastel, animation-heavy look into a restrained editorial/executive one to address the "too childish" complaint. The change swaps the purple hero gradient and peach/mint candy accents for a single steel-blue-on-paper palette (both light `:root` and the dark-mode override block), dials Playfair weights down to 600 and trims the Google Fonts request to match, deletes the looping `float`/`glow`/`pulse`/`shimmer` animations, replaces every decorative emoji with monochrome CSS marks, and tightens radii and shadows to a flat cohesive scale. It stays pure HTML+CSS with no script introduced, and all textual content is preserved verbatim. The redesign is coherent across palette, motion, iconography, typography, and shape — nothing is left half-restyled.

Watch for: dark-mode `--secondary`/`--secondary-dark` are inverted (dark value lighter than base) but the vars are dead, so harmless (confirmed); a handful of accent/secondary custom properties are now unreferenced dead tokens (confirmed); achievement/fact/learning icon spans are now purely decorative CSS bars with empty `aria-hidden` content, which is the correct a11y outcome (confirmed).

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The palette rework is the core of the change and it is complete: every old candy literal (`#667eea`, `#764ba2`, `#E8A87C`, `#85CDCA` and the `#6C8EBF` family) is gone from the stylesheet, replaced by a slate/steel-blue family driven through the existing custom properties, and the dark-mode block was updated in lockstep so both themes carry the new identity.

The CSS-only interaction model is untouched where it matters. `#dark-mode-toggle` and `#nav-toggle` remain direct siblings of `.navbar`, `main`, `.mobile-nav-panel`, and `footer`, so the `~` general-sibling selectors that power dark mode and the hamburger menu still resolve. No wrapper divs were introduced.

Iconography moved from color emoji to monochrome CSS marks applied consistently across achievements, fun facts, and learning cards, with the oversized testimonial glyph shrunk and muted. The emoji spans are emptied and marked `aria-hidden`, so they read as decoration rather than content.

Typography and the font request are consistent: no `font-weight` above 600 remains anywhere, and the trimmed Google Fonts `<link>` (Inter 300–600, Playfair 400–600) no longer requests weights the CSS can't use.

The known gaps are cosmetic only: several accent/secondary custom properties survive as aliases that nothing references, and one dark-mode token pair is inverted but unused. Neither affects rendering.

### Palette migration through the existing custom-property system

The redesign routes entirely through the `:root` tokens rather than touching individual rules, which is why it lands coherently. `--primary` becomes `#2f4a63` steel-blue, the accent and secondary families collapse into aliases of the same slate range, backgrounds move to a warm paper base (`#f6f5f2`/`#eceae5`), and the four project-banner `gradient-1..4` classes are recolored from blue/green/mauve/lavender into one slate tonal scale. Every old candy literal is verifiably gone from the stylesheet — no `667eea`, `764ba2`, `E8A87C`, `85CDCA`, or `6C8EBF` remains. `--gradient-separator` also drops from an accent-colored bar to `transparent → --border → transparent`, keeping the separator understated.

The dark-mode override block was rewritten in step: it redeclares not just backgrounds/text/border/shadow but also `--primary*` and the `--gradient-*` set with dark-appropriate literals (lighter steel-blue `#7d97ae` accent on an ink-charcoal `#14171c` base), so the dark theme carries the new palette rather than drifting back toward the old one.

### Dead tokens and one inverted pair

The one loose end from collapsing the palette: `--accent`, `--accent-light`, `--accent-dark`, `--secondary`, and `--secondary-dark` are now defined but unreferenced. The gradients that used to consume them (`--gradient-accent`, `--gradient-warm`) were rewritten with `--primary*` or hard literals, so these tokens are dead in both themes. In the dark-mode block the pair is also inverted — `--secondary-dark` (#a4adba) is lighter than `--secondary` (#8892a0). Nothing renders from them so there's no visual bug, but the inversion would mislead anyone who later wires a rule to `--secondary-dark` expecting the darker of the two. Cleanest fix is to delete the unused tokens; if they're meant to stay as a design API, point the gradients back through them and correct the dark ordering.

### CSS-only dark mode and hamburger nav preserved

Both checkboxes sit as direct siblings of `.navbar`, `main`, `.mobile-nav-panel`, and `footer`, and the `#dark-mode-toggle:checked ~ ...` (6 selectors) and `#nav-toggle:checked ~ ...` (4 selectors) rules still match; no element was wrapped in a new container. `.dark-mode-label:hover` dropped its `scale(1.1)` + glow for a border-color change without touching the toggle mechanics.

```
body
├─ input#dark-mode-toggle   ─┐  targeted by  #dark-mode-toggle:checked ~ .navbar / main / .mobile-nav-panel / footer
├─ input#nav-toggle         ─┤  targeted by  #nav-toggle:checked ~ .mobile-nav-panel (etc.)
├─ nav.navbar               ←┤
├─ div.mobile-nav-panel     ←┤
├─ main                     ←┤
└─ footer                   ←┘
```

### Motion calmed without breaking the reduced-motion contract

`@keyframes float`, `glow`, `pulse`, and `shimmer` are deleted outright, and their consumers were rehomed: the avatar ring is now a static `--border` ring with a soft shadow (no float on the SVG, no pulsing glow), the timeline dot fills without a `scale(1.2)` pop, and every card hover lift drops from -3…-6px to a uniform -2px with flat shadows instead of neon glow. The `fadeInUp` entrance survives with reduced travel (30px → 14px) and slightly shorter duration, and the staggered `.section` delays plus the `animation-fill-mode: forwards` safety net are kept. The `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block is untouched, so the accessibility contract holds.

### Emoji-to-CSS-mark iconography, applied uniformly

Every decorative emoji is replaced by a monochrome accent mark rather than a different emoji: `achievement-icon` becomes a 3px accent vertical rule stretched to the card height, `fact-emoji` and `learning-icon` become small centered accent bars. The spans are emptied and given `aria-hidden="true"`, so screen readers skip them while the card text carries meaning. The project-stats line drops the star/bowl emoji for plain `"547 stars   118 forks"` (the 547/118 figures are preserved), and the footer `&#10024;` sparkle becomes the word `care`. The testimonial quote stays a typographic `&ldquo;` but shrinks 4rem → 2rem (and 3rem → 1.8rem at the 600px breakpoint) and mutes to `--text-muted`. The pattern is uniform across all four affected areas — no section was left with its original emoji treatment.

### Typography and font request kept in sync

Playfair display weights are pulled back to 600 (h1 800 → 600 at 2.8rem, nav-brand 700 → 600 now in `--text-primary`, stat-number switched to Playfair at 600 for an editorial numeral treatment). No `font-weight` above 600 exists anywhere in the stylesheet, and the Google Fonts `<link>` was correspondingly trimmed to Inter 300;400;500;600 and Playfair 400;500;600, dropping the previously-requested 700/800. Because the CSS no longer requests weights the font link omits, there's no risk of a synthetic-bold fallback from a stale weight mismatch. Preconnect is retained.

### Shape and surface scale unified

`--border-radius` 12px → 6px and `--border-radius-lg` 20px → 10px, and every former 50px pill (cta, tag, social-btn, back-to-top) and 20px badge/tag radius now references `var(--border-radius)` — no pill/rounded literals remain except `border-radius: 50%` on genuinely circular elements (avatar ring, toggle, testimonial avatar, timeline dot). The three skill-tag variants (`tag-primary`/`tag-accent`/`tag-secondary`) collapse into one neutral treatment with a shared accent hover, so the skills section no longer reads as three candy colors. All three variant class names are still present in the markup and still matched by the combined selector, so nothing is orphaned in either direction.

### Content preservation and scope

All required text survives verbatim: name "Kuet Tai Yong", stats 12+/35K+/518/5, all 5 timeline entries, all 8 achievements, all 3 testimonials with authors James Lee / Sarah Ahmad / Raj Kumar, all 6 fun facts, all 4 learning cards, all 4 project cards with their GitHub hrefs, the three social links, and the footer "#GivetoGain" and "Built with … Women In Tech Workshop" lines. The `@media (max-width: 900px)` and `(max-width: 600px)` blocks and the `@supports not (backdrop-filter)` fallback are all present, and the 600px testimonial-quote override was updated to match the new glyph size. No `<script>`, framework, or inline handler was added. Only `site1/index.html` and `site1/style.css` changed among product files; `site2/` and `site3/` are untouched.
